# Problem 001

**Source:** SVSU Math Olympics 2022, Level I

**Difficulty:** ★★★☆☆

**Field / Subfield:** Algebra — Quadratic Equations

**Keywords / Tags:** quadratic equations, symmetric coefficients, Vieta's formulas

---

## Problem Statement

Consider the equation $p(x): ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ whose coefficients $a$, $b$, and $c$ are all non-zero, and each of them satisfies an equation that results from removing the term containing that coefficient from the equation $p(x)$. For example, the coefficient $b$ is a solution of the equation $ax^2 + c = 0$.

What is the sum of all solutions of $p(x)$?

(a) Always 1
(b) Always −1
(c) Always 2
(d) 1 or −1
(e) 1 or 2

---

## Notes

- $a$ is a root of $bx + c = 0$
- $b$ is a root of $ax^2 + c = 0$
- $c$ is a root of $ax^2 + bx = 0$
