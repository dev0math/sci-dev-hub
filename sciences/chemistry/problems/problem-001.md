## Problem Statement

A rigid, sealed 10.0 L container holds 2.00 mol of $H_2(g)$ and 3.00 mol of $O_2(g)$ at an initial temperature of 300 K.

A spark ignites the mixture, and the reaction

$$
2H_2(g) + O_2(g) \rightarrow 2H_2O(g)
$$

goes to completion, consuming the limiting reactant entirely. Assume the water produced remains entirely in the gas phase. After the reaction, the temperature inside the container is measured to be 600 K.

Using the ideal gas law ($R = 0.08206\ \text{L·atm/(mol·K)}$):

(a) Determine which reactant is limiting.
(b) Determine the total moles of gas remaining in the container after the reaction.
(c) Calculate the final total pressure inside the container, in atm.

---

## Notes

Assume ideal gas behavior throughout, and that the container volume does not change.
